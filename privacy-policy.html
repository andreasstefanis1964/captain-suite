## 2. What personal data do we collect?

Depending on how you interact with us, we may collect the following categories of personal data:

### A. Information you provide to us

When you contact us, make an enquiry, request information, or make a booking, we may collect:

* first and last name;
* email address;
* telephone number;
* postal or billing address, where required;
* booking and reservation details;
* arrival and departure dates;
* number of guests;
* requests or preferences relating to your stay;
* communications you send to us;
* information required to process or administer your booking or payment.

### B. Payment information

If you make a booking or payment through the Website, payment may be processed using third-party payment providers, including **Stripe** and/or **PayPal**, or by bank transfer.

We generally do not need to receive or store your full payment-card number. Payment-card information may be collected and processed directly by the relevant payment provider in accordance with its own privacy policy and security practices.

We may receive limited payment-related information, such as the payment status, transaction reference, amount, currency and other information necessary to reconcile and administer your booking.

### C. Technical and usage information

When you visit the Website, certain technical information may be collected automatically, depending on the cookies and other technologies enabled on the Website. This may include:

* IP address;
* browser type and version;
* operating system;
* device information;
* approximate location derived from IP address;
* pages visited;
* date and time of access;
* referring website;
* technical logs and security information.

The exact information collected depends on the technologies and third-party services active on the Website.

### D. Information relating to access to the property

For security and operational purposes, we may process information associated with electronic access systems used at Captain Suite, including the **Nuki smart-lock system** and the electronic access code used to enter the building or apartment.

Depending on the configuration of these systems, this may include information such as access credentials, access events, dates and times of entry, and technical identifiers.

Access information is used primarily to provide secure access to the accommodation, administer your stay, maintain security, and investigate security incidents where necessary.

### E. Video doorbell

Captain Suite uses a video doorbell at the **main entrance of the apartment/building** for security purposes.

The system may process video images, audio and/or information associated with an interaction with the doorbell. The camera is intended to monitor the entrance area and **not the private interior of the accommodation**.

Video surveillance is used for legitimate security purposes, including protecting guests, visitors, property and the accommodation and preventing or investigating incidents.

We will seek to configure the system so that it captures only the area reasonably necessary for these purposes.

Where required by applicable law, appropriate signage or other information will be provided in the area covered by the system.

## 4. Legal bases for processing

Under the GDPR, we process personal data only where we have an appropriate legal basis.

Depending on the circumstances, these legal bases may include:

### Performance of a contract – Article 6(1)(b) GDPR

We may process personal data where this is necessary to enter into or perform a contract with you, including:

* handling a booking;
* administering your accommodation;
* communicating with you about your reservation;
* processing or administering payments;
* providing access to the accommodation.

### Legal obligation – Article 6(1)(c) GDPR

We may process personal data where necessary to comply with a legal obligation, including applicable accounting, tax, financial, regulatory or other legal requirements.

### Legitimate interests – Article 6(1)(f) GDPR

We may process personal data where necessary for our legitimate interests, provided those interests are not overridden by your fundamental rights and freedoms.

These interests may include:

* maintaining Website and information-security;
* preventing fraud and misuse;
* protecting our property and guests;
* operating electronic access systems;
* securing the accommodation;
* investigating security incidents;
* establishing or defending legal claims;
* managing and improving our business.

### Consent – Article 6(1)(a) GDPR

Where required by law, we will ask for your consent before processing personal data for a particular purpose, such as certain non-essential cookies or direct marketing.

You may withdraw consent at any time. Withdrawal of consent does not affect the lawfulness of processing carried out before withdrawal.

## 8. How long do we keep your personal data?

We retain personal data only for as long as necessary for the purposes for which it was collected, unless a longer period is required or permitted by law.

Different categories of information may therefore have different retention periods.

For example:

* **Booking and guest information** may be retained for as long as necessary to administer your reservation and subsequently for the period required for accounting, tax, legal and dispute-resolution purposes.
* **Payment and accounting records** may be retained for the period required by applicable financial, accounting and tax legislation.
* **Enquiry and communication records** may be retained for as long as reasonably necessary to handle the enquiry and for legitimate business or legal purposes.
* **Electronic access records** should be retained only for as long as reasonably necessary for security, operational and legal purposes.
* **Video-doorbell recordings**, where recordings are made, should be retained only for the shortest period reasonably necessary for security purposes and any applicable legal requirements. Where an incident occurs, relevant footage may be retained for longer where necessary to investigate the incident or establish, exercise or defend legal claims.

We periodically review retained personal data and delete or anonymise information that is no longer required.

## 9. Your rights under the GDPR

Subject to applicable legal conditions and exceptions, you have the following rights:

### Right of access

You may ask us whether we process personal data about you and request a copy of that personal data.

### Right to rectification

You may ask us to correct inaccurate or incomplete personal data.

### Right to erasure

You may ask us to delete your personal data where there is no longer a lawful reason for us to retain it.

This right is not absolute. We may need to retain certain information to comply with legal obligations or establish, exercise or defend legal claims.

### Right to restriction of processing

You may ask us to restrict the processing of your personal data in certain circumstances.

### Right to data portability

Where applicable, you may request personal data that you have provided to us in a structured, commonly used and machine-readable format and ask us to transmit it to another controller.

### Right to object

You may object to processing based on our legitimate interests where you have grounds relating to your particular situation.

You also have the right to object at any time to processing of personal data for direct marketing purposes.

### Right to withdraw consent

Where processing is based on consent, you may withdraw your consent at any time.
